## How to record

* **Say the reference text.** Read it once silently, then say it naturally, the way you would
  complain to a neighbour, not in a reading voice. Small slips are fine, but if you change or
  skip a word, record again: the text is what your clip is scored against.
* **Quiet clips:** indoors, phone 20–30 cm from your mouth.
* **Noisy clips:** real background noise. Use traffic at a window or on the street, a fan on
  full, or a TV or music in the room. Do not add noise afterwards.
* **Length:** 5–30 seconds. The app rejects clips under 1 s or over 60 s, or that are mostly silence.
* **App:** the phone's own voice recorder, or a WhatsApp voice note sent to yourself.
  Keep the file the phone produced (`.m4a`, `.ogg`, `.opus`, `.mp3`, `.wav`, `.aac`).
  Do not convert, trim or enhance it.
* **Consent:** by recording you agree to the clip being stored in the team's repository and
  used for this evaluation. Never say a real name, phone number or address.

## File names

Rename each file to:

    <speaker_id>_<test_id>_<condition>.<ext>
    <speaker_id>_<test_id>_<condition>_<device>.<ext>      (optional device)

* `condition` is one lowercase word for what was actually around you: `quiet`, `street`,
  `traffic`, `fan`, `tv`, `music` or `crowd`. Use `quiet` for every clip that has no noise.
* `device` is one lowercase word, e.g. `android`, `iphone`, `whatsapp` or `laptop`. If you leave it out it is
  recorded as `phone`.

Examples: `S02_T034_quiet.m4a`, `S03_T107_street_whatsapp.ogg`.

Send the 12 files to Aarush (a shared folder is easiest), not by WhatsApp chat, which re-compresses them.

## For Aarush: building the manifest

Copy all clips into `data/audio/` (the folder is git-ignored), then run:

    python eval/recordings.py manifest

This writes `data/audio/manifest.csv` with the columns `eval/asr_eval.py` reads (`file,
reference_text, department, locality, speaker_id, condition, device, language`, plus
`test_id`), taking the reference text from the assignments. It lists every clip still missing,
by speaker, and skips any file whose name does not match. Fix the names and run it again.
Then run Phase 1 (`python eval/asr_eval.py`).

`eval/recording_assignments.csv` is generated by `python eval/recordings.py assign` (fixed
seed). It picks 48 of the 100 Marathi and Hindi `test_v1` rows, 4 or 5 per department,
12 of them Hindi. Do not regenerate it once people have started recording.

## Why test_v1 sentences

`data/audio/README.md` originally said not to record test-set sentences, so that ASR errors
are not mixed up with what the classifier has memorised. We record them anyway so that each
clip can be compared with the same complaint typed. See `docs/DECISIONS.md` R9.
